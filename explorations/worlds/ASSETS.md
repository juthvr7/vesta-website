### Final prompt: world hero

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic wide website hero background for Vesta, an AI-native world creation platform in development. Create one exceptionally art-directed 2048x1152 landscape image, no text or UI. Scene: a vast horseshoe-shaped limestone canyon, immense beautifully terraced cliffs descending into a winding luminous jade-green river, lush moss and dark pine forest over plateau edges, a narrow stone bridge crossing a chasm, a small coherent warm-lit hill settlement tucked into the middle-right cliffs for human scale, one distant waterfall dissolving into pale mist. Far mountains and dramatic overcast evening sky, one precise shaft of warm low sunlight grazing rock faces. Camera: elevated aerial establishing view looking INTO the landscape, not a top-down map or floating diorama; cinematic 28mm lens, strong foreground/middle/background depth. Compose the most memorable landscape and settlement in the RIGHT TWO THIRDS. Keep the LEFT THIRD lower detail and deep shadowed forest/rock for large white website copy, but no blank flat panel. The river leads the eye from lower middle to distant right. Style: premium physically credible offline 3D environment render, sophisticated geological detail, quiet epic science-fiction worldbuilding without spaceships, tasteful film color grading, tactile rock, light atmospheric grain. Palette: deep charcoal green, jade water, pale limestone, small amber windows, warm sunlight; no neon, purple, magical glowing effects, oversaturated orange, or plastic game art. It should feel like a place a visitor wants to explore and shape, with a striking readable silhouette even as a thumbnail. Constraints: no letters, words, numbers, logos, watermark, grid, interface frames, planets, fantasy towers, people in foreground, or collage. One coherent cinematic world. This is concept imagery for an aspiration section and will be labeled as concept, not claimed as software output.

## Actual Vesta output films

The three films (plus a portrait layout of the same comparison for mobile) are
generated by `render-films.py`, using the immutable CLI result
pair identified by `../atlas/assets/provenance.json`. Six seconds, 18 frames per
second, H.264/yuv420p. Shared camera and 20% display relief. The rendering mesh
samples every fourth source sample on each axis. Colors are presentation shading,
not generated vegetation or materials. The first two films orbit Ridge and Terraces;
the third places the same frames side by side. No source samples are changed.

These clips are not live operation recordings, erosion simulations over time,
performance claims or recordings of the future Builder interface.
