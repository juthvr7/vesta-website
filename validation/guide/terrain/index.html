    Terrain

    Terrain is the first domain in Vesta’s world-creation path. Browse it by the job you need to do: generate a starting form, reshape its structure, simulate change or control local influence.

    Starts with
    A bounded heightfield, seed and parameters; optional masks or layers.
    Produces
    Editable terrain data that can continue through the same Graph and asset workflow.
